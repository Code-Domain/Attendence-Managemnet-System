About the project

Student Attendance Tracker

A shared attendance workspace for your organization.

For an organization

The system is designed for a school or college to keep students, cohorts, teachers and attendance in one shared service.

Separate responsibilities

Administrators manage records, subjects and teacher accounts. Teachers work with their own assigned classes.

Online database

Both workspaces use the same MySQL database, so authorized users see current organization data.

The thinking behind it

Attendance should be dependable and easy to follow.

The workspace follows a few simple principles that keep everyday school operations clear.

Start with the right roster

Classes and subject assignments define who belongs on each attendance list.

Give each role a clear view

Administrators manage organization records. Teachers focus on assigned classes.

Keep a useful history

Recorded attendance stays available for review and date-range reporting.

A connected workflow

One organization. One shared record.

Student, subject and attendance data come together to support consistent record keeping across the team.
